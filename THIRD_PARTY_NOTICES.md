The code, text, tables, figures and results written by the authors are released under the MIT licence ([LICENSE](LICENSE)). The material below was not written by them. The licences are those listed upstream when we looked in October 2026; please check the source before you redistribute anything.

## Included in this repository

| Item | Where | Terms |
|---|---|---|
| ACL style file `acl.sty` | `paper/acl.sty` | Unmodified copy from the official ACL style files (<https://github.com/acl-org/acl-style-files>). The file states no licence of its own; see the upstream repository. |
| ACL bibliography style `acl_natbib.bst` | `paper/acl_natbib.bst` | Unmodified copy from the same source. Its header says that it derives from the natbib style `plainnat` (Patrick W. Daly) through urlbst (Norman Gray) and is distributed under the LaTeX Project Public License. |
| Correctness labels of the Monkey Business release | `results/04_checkpoints/monkey_business/*.npz` | Taken from the dataset `ScalingIntelligence/monkey_business` (Brown et al., 2024), listed under the MIT licence. Only the 0/1 labels, the number of attempts per problem and a record of the download are kept; the generations are not included. The attempts were generated by Llama-3-8B-Instruct, Llama-3-70B-Instruct, Gemma-7B and Pythia-12B; we have not assessed whether the terms of those models say anything about derived labels. |
| Outcomes of sampling with Qwen2.5-family models | `results/04_checkpoints/samples/*.jsonl` | Produced by the authors with Qwen2.5-0.5B, Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct, Qwen2.5-1.5B (Qwen team) and Qwen-2.5-1.5B-SimpleRL-Zoo (`hkust-nlp`), all listed under the Apache-2.0 licence. The files hold 0/1 outcomes, lengths, extracted final answers (at most 80 characters) and the last 32 characters of each generation, not the generations. |
| Indices of the problems used | `results/04_checkpoints/gsm8k_subset.pkl`, `math500_order.pkl` | Indices into the public GSM8K test split and the MATH-500 split. No problem text is included. |
