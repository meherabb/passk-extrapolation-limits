# Known issues

Things that do not line up perfectly between the paper, the notebook and the archive, and what the checks in this repository leave out. None of them changes a number in the paper.

## Names and files that differ from the paper

- **Working title.** The notebook and the archive were produced under an earlier working title, "A Quadratic Horizon and a Doubling Barrier: Distribution-Free Limits on Extrapolating pass@k from Repeated Samples". It is still in the first cell of both notebooks and in `STUDY_TITLE` (cell 5), in the names of the archives the notebook writes (`DoublingBarrier-QuadraticHorizon_passk-extrapolation_results_…`) and in four files of `results/`: `README.md`, `run_manifest.json`, `key_results_summary.txt` and `05_environment/environment_and_config.json`. Those files are shipped as they were written, because their checksums are part of the record. The paper's title is the one at the top of the [README](../README.md).
- **Numbering.** Figures and tables in `results/` are numbered by the notebook (`Fig01` to `Fig29`, `Tab01` to `Tab22`); the paper numbers its own. [paper-to-output-map.md](paper-to-output-map.md) gives the correspondence.
- **`results/README.md`** was generated before several figures and tables were added. It lists `Fig01` to `Fig22` and `Tab01` to `Tab13` and calls two folders empty; the archive holds `Fig01` to `Fig29` and `Tab01` to `Tab22`.
- **Generated drafts.** `results/abstract_draft.txt`, `results/paper_outline.md` and the abstract printed near the end of the notebook were generated from the numbers of the run. The abstract and outline of the paper are in `paper/main.tex`.
- **Superseded stream.** M-I0.5 (Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct on MATH-500, 512-token cap) truncated 48.5% of its attempts and was replaced by M-I0.5L (1,024 tokens). Its samples are kept, and the paper marks it as superseded.

## What the checks cover, and what they do not

- **`make paper-numbers`** compares five tables of the paper (error floor, ground truth, certified horizons, stress tests, claims ledger) and 66 statements and numbers quoted in the text with the result files. The other appendix tables are not compared by a script. They were typeset from the CSV tables, and we checked once, with a throwaway script that is not part of this repository, that every number printed in them occurs in the corresponding table. That check looks for presence, not for position, so it would not notice two swapped cells.
- **`make theory`** recomputes constants and values (for example 2*E*<sub>*n*</sub>(*x*<sup>*k*</sup>)) numerically, by linear programming. It does not check the proofs.
- **`make anonymity`** finds what its patterns and the strings you give it describe. It reads the bytes of every file, the metadata of PDF and PNG files and the members of archives; it does not read text that is drawn inside a figure.
- **`make verify`** has been run on Python 3.13 with the versions in `requirements.txt`. The workflow in `.github/workflows/ci.yml` runs the same command on every push; it can only run on GitHub, so we could not test it. Other Python versions will probably work but have not been tried.

## Reproduction

The details are in [reproducing.md](reproducing.md). In short:

- the PDF figures are not byte-reproducible (matplotlib writes a creation date), so the data behind them are compared;
- after recomputing the 31 stages of the final run, 72 numbers in three figure-data files differ from the shipped ones by up to 0.5%, because the stages end an iterative solver at a tolerance; no table entry changed;
- recomputing all analysis stages (`--drop-all-caches`) and repeating the sampling on a GPU have not been run by us;
- the first notebook cell installs a package with `pip` if numpy, scipy, pandas, matplotlib or mpmath is missing or older than its minimum.

## Paper sources

- The link in the abstract ends in `XXXX`; it is a placeholder for the suffix of the anonymous mirror of this repository.
- The LaTeX class files need the `inconsolata` package. It was not installed on the machine where we built the paper, so our test build used an empty stand-in for it, and line breaks around monospaced text may differ slightly from a build with the real font. See [../paper/README.md](../paper/README.md).
- `paper/acl.sty` and `paper/acl_natbib.bst` are unmodified copies of the official ACL files and keep their own terms (see [../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md)).

## Data

- Licences are given as listed upstream in October 2026. The dataset card of the `HuggingFaceH4/MATH-500` mirror states none, although MATH and PRM800K, the sources of that split, are listed under the MIT licence.
- The Monkey Business labels were generated by Llama-3, Gemma and Pythia models. We include only the 0/1 correctness labels and have not assessed whether the terms of those models say anything about derived labels.
